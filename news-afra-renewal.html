AFRA and Block Aero Renew Partnership for Five Years

A five-year renewal — and delivery of the AFRA-CAAC asset registry solution to the CAAC and its stakeholders in the aviation supply chain.

Block Aero and the Aircraft Fleet Recycling Association (AFRA) have renewed their partnership for five years and delivered the AFRA-CAAC asset registry solution to the Civil Aviation Administration of China (CAAC) and its stakeholders across the aviation supply chain.

The renewal extends a collaboration that began with blockchain-backed used-parts registries and became the AFRA-CAAC Registry program. Block Aero is the program's implementation partner and operates the only overseas compliant parts management platform for the program.

The registry records aircraft (MSN), engines (ESN), and dismantled parts (PART) so used serviceable material recovered from retired airframes can carry a verifiable trace toward China-registered fleets. Delivery of the solution puts that record in the hands of the CAAC and the organizations that depend on it.

The program is in a voluntary participation phase under CAAC Advisory Circular AC-145-FS-017 R1. A Dismantle Parts Tag generated through the program documents the harvest and its traceability; it is not an Authorized Release Certificate.

The original AFRA partnership was announced in March 2024, following the December 2023 US–China USM Sales Seminar in Phoenix.
