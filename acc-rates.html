Aero Compute Credits — Rate Schedule

Aero Compute Credits (ACC) are the unified metering unit for everything you do on the Block Aero platform — blockchain operations today, AI services as they reach general availability. One transparent unit, one published price list. 1 ACC = $0.01 USD.

Effective 1 July 2026 · Rates are updated from time to time; material changes take effect per your agreement and Order Form. Prices in USD, exclusive of applicable taxes. This schedule is referenced by the Block Aero General Terms Agreement; in any conflict, your Order Form prevails.

How ACC Works

Three Rules, No Surprises

1 ACC = $0.01

Every plan includes a monthly ACC allowance — Starter 500,000 · Professional 1,750,000 · Enterprise 5,000,000. Top up anytime or step up a tier. See plans

Volume is rewarded

Batch operations above 100 items earn a 20% discount on batch-eligible rates — registering thousands of parts from a teardown scales efficiently.

Transfers earn rebates

Executing an ownership transfer is free — and triggers an ACC rebate of up to 50% of the credits spent enriching the transferred assets, capped at 20% of your monthly allocation.

Always Free

Safety and Provenance Are Never Metered

The records that matter most carry no ACC cost — there is no barrier to capturing the full story of an asset or complying with airworthiness requirements.
