The Problem

Aviation Runs on Trust. Trust Still Runs on Paper.

Every hour your fleet flies is backed by records — and today those records live in boxes, scanned PDFs, and disconnected systems spread across owners, operators, and shops. Every transition, audit, and sale starts with the same question: can you prove it?

Records Scatter

Every shop visit, lease, and change of ownership spreads your asset's history across more systems, more formats, and more hands.

Transitions Drag

Redeliveries and asset sales stall while your teams chase, re-check, and rebuild 8130-3s and trace records by hand — at the most expensive moment in the deal.

Value Leaks

A gap in back-to-birth traceability means discounts, disputes, and write-downs. Records you can't prove become value you can't recover.
