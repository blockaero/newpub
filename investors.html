Why Block Aero

Adopted by Customers. Aligned with Regulators. Certified.

The compliant path into China

Block Aero is the implementation partner of the AFRA-CAAC Registry and the only overseas compliant parts management platform — securing used serviceable material traceability from teardown to installation on China-registered aircraft. The program is in its voluntary participation phase, aligned with the CAAC's Advisory Circular on aircraft disassembly.

Real commercial adoption

Customers across the supply chain manage their assets on the Aviation Blockchain Network — including Unical, eCube, AAR, MD Turbines, JALUX, Stratton Aviation, CAVU Aerospace, and Turbo Resources.

Institution-grade trust

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified (ASA CB, BLOC-001-06-26-27), aligned with the ICAO Trust Framework, and partnered with AFRA, the CAAC, ICAO, ASA CB, and JALUX/JAL.
