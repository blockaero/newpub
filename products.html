Digital Passports

Every MSN, ESN, and PART Gets a Blockchain-Endorsed Identity

Digital Asset Manager gives every aircraft, engine, APU, and serialized part one complete, governed digital record — a digital passport issued on the Block Aero Aviation Blockchain Network. When provenance is provable on demand, your assets hold their residual value and your next transition, audit, or sale doesn't stall on missing paperwork.

  • Back-to-birth chain of custody that survives ownership changes, company closures, and IT migrations.
  • Lifecycle event tracking across ownership changes, leases, and shop visits — with airworthiness compliance and eARC / e8130-3 issuance.
  • Role-based access for owners, operators, and asset managers — your partners see what you allow, nothing more.
  • Seamless asset transfers between stakeholders: the passport travels, the history stays intact.
  • AI-assisted records management — classify unstructured PDFs against the 108-type Data Sharing Specification with human-in-the-loop approval before anything is committed on-chain.

Data Package Creation

The slowest part of every USM deal is assembling the proof. Data Package Creation lets you pull verified records, certificates, and photos for any asset into a standardized, shareable data package — and send it to buyers, regulators, OEMs, and lessors in hours instead of weeks.

  • Assemble in clicks: select an MSN, ESN, or PART and its verified documents follow automatically.
  • Share anywhere: off-network customers and suppliers receive packages through secure links — no Block Aero account required.
  • Standardized and verifiable: every document is cryptographically hashed and traceable to the asset's digital passport.
  • #RegistryReady: packages align to registry requirements like AFRA-CAAC, so buyer due diligence takes minutes, not weeks.

Flagship Program

The AFRA-CAAC Registry

Block Aero is the implementation partner of the AFRA-CAAC Registry — and the only overseas compliant parts management platform. AFRA, the CAAC, CCAR-145 Disassembly Service Providers, and Asset Owners cooperate on Block Aero to secure traceability from teardown to serviceable material that can be installed on China-registered aircraft. The program runs in a voluntary participation phase under the CAAC's Advisory Circular on aircraft disassembly.

Read about the AFRA-CAAC program →

Aircraft Registry · MSN Engine Registry · ESN Dismantled Parts Registry · PART
  • Accredited teardown: disassembly events recorded at the source by CCAR-145 limited disassembly facilities.
  • Endorsed data packages: every registry item carries verified documents and photos, audited under the program.
  • Buyer confidence in China: airlines and MROs verify eligibility against the registry in minutes.
