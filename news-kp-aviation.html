KP Aviation Joins the Aviation Blockchain Network

Welcoming a global aftermarket and parts-supply company onto the network.

KP Aviation is joining the Aviation Blockchain Network.

KP Aviation is a global aftermarket and parts-supply company. Kim Schulze, co-founder of KP Aviation, joined Block Aero's Board in June 2025. She has spent over two decades in aftermarket material and asset management, growing KP Aviation from a boutique supplier into an international company with facilities in Arizona, Europe, and South Africa across teardowns, engine leasing, and inventory distribution.

We are glad to welcome KP Aviation onto the network.
