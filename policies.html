Privacy Policy

VERSION 1.0 · EFFECTIVE 2026-06-12 · OWNER: CEO · NEXT REVIEW: 2027-06

Who we are. Block Aero Technologies (Thailand) Ltd, 38, 5-6 Soi Sukhumvit 39, Khlong Tan Nuea, Watthana, Bangkok 10110, Thailand ("Block Aero", "we") is the data controller for personal data processed through this website and in our business operations, in accordance with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and applicable data protection laws where we operate.

What we collect

  • Business contact data — name, work email, organization, role, and phone number when you book a demo, register investor interest, contact support, or attend our events.
  • Account data — credentials and organization details when your organization activates a Block Aero account via our account management application.
  • Usage data — log and device information needed to operate, secure, and improve this website and our services.

Why we process it

  • To perform contracts with customer organizations and operate the Block Aero platform and Aviation Blockchain Network.
  • To respond to inquiries, demos, support requests, and investor-relations registrations (legitimate interest and/or consent).
  • To meet legal, regulatory, and information-security obligations, including aviation customer contractual requirements.

Sharing and transfers

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers under data processing agreements (assessed under our supplier security program), with authorities where the law requires, and within transactions you ask us to perform. Where data crosses borders, we apply PDPA-compliant safeguards.

Retention and your rights

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as law requires. Under the PDPA you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection, and you may withdraw consent at any time. Contact support@block.aero — we respond to verified requests within 30 days. You may lodge a complaint with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

A note on blockchain records: asset records on the Aviation Blockchain Network describe aircraft, engines, and parts — not people. Where business documents contain personal data (e.g., a signatory name on a certificate), originals are stored off-chain under access control; the chain stores cryptographic hashes.

Information Security Policy (Public Summary)

VERSION 1.0 · EFFECTIVE 2026-06-12 · OWNER: CTO/COO · NEXT REVIEW: 2027-06

Block Aero operates an information security management system certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 by the Aviation Suppliers Association Certification Body (Certificate BLOC-001-06-26-27), covering the design, development, and maintenance of the Block Aero platform for data digitization.

  • Governance: executive-owned ISMS with management reviews, internal audit program, and an annually refreshed Statement of Applicability.
  • Access control: role-based, least-privilege access for customers and staff; joiner/mover/leaver reviews.
  • Data protection: encryption in transit and at rest; segregation between sandbox, production, and private channels.
  • Blockchain integrity: permissioned Hyperledger Fabric network with endorsement policies controlling who may write which records.
  • Resilience: continuity and disaster-recovery exercises and independent penetration testing on an annual cadence.
  • Suppliers: tiered vendor security assessments and data processing agreements for providers that touch customer data.
  • Incidents: a documented incident response process with root-cause analysis and corrective action tracking.

Report a vulnerability or security concern: support@block.aero. See live compliance status in the Trust Center.
