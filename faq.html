Products & Pricing

What You Can Run on Block Aero

What is the Digital Asset Manager?

The Digital Asset Manager is our core product — a monthly or annual subscription that digitizes your records and assets into verified digital passports. It includes AI-assisted records management and Data Package Creation, so your team can assemble and share trusted records from one place. Explore the Digital Asset Manager

What is the Registry Manager?

Registry Manager is an add-on module that delivers Registry as a Service. It supports the roles a registry needs — Registry Managers, Auditors, and Registry Item Issuers — to operate compliant registries on the network. Explore the Registry Manager

How is the Digital Asset Manager priced?

Pricing is an Organization License — monthly or annual — plus per-asset usage fees, with service levels SL I through SL IV to match your volume. Every active organization's baseline is the Block Aero Core Platform Subscription plus Aviation Blockchain Network access in both sandbox and production.

What is the AI Records Manager?

AI Records Manager is an Early Access product that ingests unstructured records — scanned PDFs, mixed-quality scans, and tags — and classifies them by document type, from FAA Form 8130-3s and EASA Form 1s to certificates of conformity and shop reports. It extracts key fields such as part number, serial number, CAGE code, and TSN/CSN, structures the results to the Data Sharing Specification (DSS), and flags trace gaps and cross-document inconsistencies for review. It runs on Tessaron, Block Aero's meta-model for aviation documents, and keeps a human in the loop before anything is committed. You can start free with up to 50 assets, or take ARM Pro at $499/month for unlimited assets — document packages and reports are $3.00 each. ARM is included in every Core subscription (SL I–III); ARM-only plans don't include the blockchain network. Learn more about ARM · Start free at ai-records-manager.com

What is the AI QC Manager?

AI QC Manager is an Early Access product that runs an AI-assisted quality-control review of records and the part imagery that accompanies them — data plates, status tags, and markings. It checks that fields and markings line up across documents and photos, flagging gaps or mismatches before they surface in a deal or an audit. As an Early Access product, it is being rolled out to a limited set of organizations. Learn more (Early Access)

How does Block Aero handle records like 8130-3s and EASA Form 1?

Authorized release documents — FAA Form 8130-3, EASA Form 1, and CAAC AAC-038 — are among the document types the Digital Asset Manager's AI-assisted records management classifies against the Data Sharing Specification (DSS). For scanned, mixed-quality, or otherwise unstructured versions of these records, AI Records Manager (Early Access) extends that classification, extracts fields such as part number, serial number, and dates, and flags trace gaps or cross-document inconsistencies for review. Learn more (Early Access)

Do I need the Registry Manager to use Block Aero?

No. The Digital Asset Manager is the core product and stands on its own. Registry Manager is an optional add-on module for organizations that need to operate a registry as a service. Compare the products

Digital Passports & the Network

The Aviation Blockchain Network

What is the Aviation Blockchain Network (ABN)?

The Aviation Blockchain Network is the shared network where your verified records live. It's the foundation that makes a record trustworthy across organizations — your assets, your proof, owned by you. See how the network works

What is a digital passport (BAA)?

A Blockchain Aviation Asset (BAA) is the on-chain record for an asset — its digital passport. Digital passports are issued on the Block Aero Aviation Blockchain Network and travel with the asset across its life. Learn about digital passports

Who owns my data?

You do. Your records and assets remain yours. The network lets you control what you share and with whom, and gives counterparties a way to verify what you've shared without handing over your whole archive.

What is the Data Sharing Specification?

The Data Sharing Specification (DSS) is the standard that organizes records into 108 document types. AI-assisted records management in the Digital Asset Manager classifies unstructured PDFs to that 108-type DSS standard, so your records are consistent and searchable. See the Data Sharing Specification

What is the Trust List?

The Trust List is how organizations and roles are recognized on the network. It is aligned with the ICAO Trust Framework and ISO 27001 guidelines, so trust on the network maps to recognized industry standards. View the Trust List

Can I share records with companies that aren't on the network?

Yes. Data Package Creation lets you assemble verified records into shareable packages — and those packages can be shared with off-network counterparties such as buyers, regulators, OEMs, and lessors. See Data Package Creation

USM & Data Packages

Move Used Serviceable Material Faster

What is Data Package Creation?

Data Package Creation is a flagship feature of the Digital Asset Manager. It lets you assemble verified records into a single shareable package for buyers, regulators, OEMs, and lessors — turning a job that used to take weeks into one that takes hours. Explore Data Packages

How does this help a USM sale?

Buyers can verify what they're purchasing instead of waiting on records to be chased down and reassembled. A complete, verified package removes the back-and-forth and the doubt that slow deals and erode value.

What is back-to-birth traceability and why does it matter?

Back-to-birth traceability is a complete, unbroken records trail for a part — covering every install, removal, repair, and ownership change from original manufacture to today. It matters because that history is what establishes a life-limited part's remaining life and what lets a buyer, lessor, or regulator trust that the records in front of them actually belong to the part. Assembling and verifying that trail is central to Data Package Creation, and it's one of the gaps AI Records Manager (Early Access) is built to flag. See more terms in the glossary

Can I list assets on marketplaces?

Yes. Through openAPI Integration Services you can list assets on marketplaces such as ILS with immutable proof-of-ownership links attached, so buyers get verification in minutes. See the integrations

Do you have proof this works at scale?

JALUX, a Japanese aviation multinational, runs its supply chain on Block Aero — 13,753 repair orders, $100M+ in transaction volume, and 150,000+ assets managed on the network. It's a concrete example of the platform under real enterprise load. See solutions by role

Can Block Aero help with lease redeliveries?

Yes. A lease redelivery typically comes down to a complete records package — proof of AD/SB compliance, life-limited part status, and back-to-birth traceability — that satisfies the lessor's return conditions. Data Package Creation lets you assemble that package from your verified records, and AI QC Manager (Early Access) can help review records and part markings for gaps before the lessor's inspection does. The AFRA-CAAC Registry

Compliant Cross-Border USM

What is the AFRA-CAAC Registry?

The AFRA-CAAC Registry is a program in a voluntary participation phase under CAAC Advisory Circular AC-145-FS-017 R1. Block Aero is the implementation partner of the AFRA-CAAC Registry and the only overseas compliant parts management platform for it. Read about the program

Is participation required?

No. The program is currently in a voluntary participation phase. Organizations choose to participate to support compliant cross-border USM traceability into the Chinese market.

What does the registry cover?

The registry covers aircraft by serial number (MSN), engines by serial number (ESN), and dismantled parts (PART). Each registry type tracks the assets relevant to compliant cross-border movement. See the registries

Is a Dismantle Parts Tag an airworthiness release?

No. A Dismantle Parts Tag is not an Authorized Release Certificate. It is distinct from an 8130-3, an EASA Form 1, or a CAAC AAC-038 and should not be treated as a substitute for one.

Who are the program partners?

Block Aero works with AFRA and CAAC on the program, alongside relationships with bodies such as ICAO. This is how cross-border USM traceability is being built on recognized industry footing. Learn more
