Company Information

The Company Behind the Network

Block Aero builds the trust layer for aerospace — a permissioned network where every aircraft, engine, and part carries a verified digital passport. We operate as a group of companies across Asia and the Americas, working with airlines, OEMs, MROs, lessors, recyclers, regulators, and associations worldwide.

Mission Statement

A Digital Passport for Every Aerospace Asset in the World

Making records verifiable, transactions frictionless, and asset value protected for life. Safety, efficiency, and collaboration guide everything we build.

Safety

An unsafe part is almost always a documentation failure first. Verified identity and complete lifecycle history mean a part's status can be proven rather than assumed — at receiving inspection, at installation, and at audit.

Efficiency

Every party in a transaction re-verifies the same paperwork at their own expense. Anchoring identity, provenance, and transaction history once removes that duplicated checking — and the weeks of manual cross-referencing that come with it.

Collaboration

When you join, your counterparties are already here: verified airlines, MROs, OEMs, lessors, regulators, and recyclers on one permissioned network — each working from one shared, trusted record per asset.
