MD Turbines Digitizes Disassembly with AI Records Manager

MD Turbines is using Block Aero ARM to turn disassembly paperwork into verified records on the Aviation Blockchain Network.

MD Turbines is on the Aviation Blockchain Network and is using Block Aero's AI Records Manager (ARM) — the agentic records product — to digitize disassembly operations.

AI Records Manager reads the paper estate a teardown actually produces: scanned certificates, mixed-quality PDFs, tags, and handwritten entries. It classifies those documents against the Data Sharing Specification, extracts the fields that matter, and binds the result to each asset's digital passport — with a human in the loop before anything is committed on-chain.

Digitizing disassembly at the source means harvested components enter the network with a record that can travel with the part, rather than a folder that has to be rebuilt later.

MD Turbines (MDTR) is listed among the CAAC-ready facilities an asset owner can work with for a CAAC-compliant teardown on Block Aero.
