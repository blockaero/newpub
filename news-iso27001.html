Block Aero Achieves ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Certification

Independent assurance for every organization that trusts its records to the Aviation Blockchain Network.

Block Aero's information security management system (ISMS) has been certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 by the Aviation Suppliers Association Certification Body (ASA CB). The certificate — BLOC-001-06-26-27 — was issued on 11 June 2026 and is valid through 10 June 2029, subject to annual surveillance.

The certified scope covers the design, development, and maintenance of the Block Aero platform for data digitization — the systems behind the digital passports, registries, and data packages our customers rely on. Certification was assessed against the current 2022 revision of the standard, with controls documented in our Statement of Applicability (v2.0.0).

For an aviation records platform, security is not a feature — it is the product. Owners, lessors, MROs, OEMs, and regulators only benefit from a shared system of record if they can trust how that record is protected. ISO/IEC 27001 is the international benchmark for managing information security risk, and independent certification means our controls have been audited and verified by an accredited third party rather than simply asserted by us.

The certification is delivered by ASA CB, the certification body of the Aviation Suppliers Association — an assessor with deep roots in the aviation supply chain the platform serves.

Certificate at a glance

Standard: ISO/IEC 27001:2022
Certificate no.: BLOC-001-06-26-27
Certification body: Aviation Suppliers Association Certification Body (ASA CB)
Issued: 11 June 2026  ·  Valid to: 10 June 2029
Scope: Design, development and maintenance of the Block Aero platform for data digitization.

"Our customers put their most important records on Block Aero. ISO 27001 certification is how we prove — to them and to their auditors — that protecting those records is built into how we operate, not bolted on."
— Todd Siena, Founder & CEO, Block Aero
