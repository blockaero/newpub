How It Works

Pay for Activity, Not Seats

Aero Compute Credits meter what you actually do on the network. Most everyday actions draw only a small amount, so a plan's monthly allowance goes a long way.

A monthly allowance

Each plan includes a pool of ACC credits that refreshes every month. Use them across registration, enrichment, documentation, and more.

Volume is rewarded

Large batch operations — like registering thousands of parts from a teardown — receive an automatic discount, so big projects scale efficiently.

Top up or move up

Busy month? Top up credits anytime, or step up a tier. We'll help you right-size — talk to us about usage pricing.

View the Published ACC Rate Schedule

One unified credit: ACC meters blockchain operations today and AI services as they reach general availability — one balance, one published price list.
