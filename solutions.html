By Capability

What You Can Do on Block Aero

Your systems, processes, and people already work — but the connective tissue between organizations is still manual proof. These capabilities put that proof on rails, so it moves as fast as your assets do.

Digital Passports for Assets

Your assets travel the world, and their value depends on the records that follow them — but records that travel by hand arrive late, incomplete, or contested. A digital passport gives every aircraft, engine, and part a tamper-proof identity with complete lifecycle tracking, transferring seamlessly between owners, operators, and managers.

Demonstrate blockchain-endorsed compliance through standardized datasets published to registries — with private staging rooms, oversight and revocation features, and a public portal for limited verification access.

Data Management for Fleets

Your fleet generates a constant stream of operational data, but it lands in systems that don't talk to each other — so the full picture is always assembled after the fact. Block Aero lets you govern it all in one place: integrate ADS-B feeds to automate tracking of MSN movements and their installed assets, and use IoT sensor integrations to monitor ESNs in real time.

Data Package Creation

Every asset you sell travels with a mountain of proof — and assembling it is the slowest step in the deal. Data Package Creation pulls verified records, certificates, and photos into standardized packages you can share with buyers, regulators, OEMs, and lessors in hours instead of weeks. Off-network counterparties receive them through secure links — no Block Aero account required.

Registry as a Service

The industry relies on your authority to certify what's true — but paper-based registries make that truth slow to publish and hard to verify. Registry as a Service lets regulators and industry associations govern trusted digital registries: submission, publication, audit, and review of standardized aviation data, with hybrid public and private access for validating and certifying asset records. The AFRA-CAAC Registry — aircraft (MSN), engines (ESN), and dismantled parts (PART) — runs on Block Aero today.

From Unstructured Files to #RegistryReady Data

Your records hold the answers, but they arrive as scans and PDFs that only human hours can unlock. Digital Asset Manager's AI-assisted records management classifies files against the 108-type Data Sharing Specification — with human-in-the-loop approval before anything is committed to an asset's passport — so registry submissions and data packages build themselves from clean data.

Live Data for Assets on Lease & Technical Asset Management

Once an asset leaves your hands, your visibility usually leaves with it — and contractual compliance becomes a quarterly argument. Block Aero connects assets in real time, feeding environmental, logistical, and operational data directly into the digital passport, so you can demonstrate contractual compliance and monitor assets out on lease or shop visit.
