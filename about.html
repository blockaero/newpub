About Us

Building the Trust Layer for Aerospace

Your assets are only worth what their records can prove — and proving them shouldn't be a fight. We're a team of aviation, technology, and blockchain specialists working with airlines, OEMs, MROs, lessors, regulators, and associations worldwide to make sure it never is.

Our Mission

To create a digital passport for every aerospace asset in the world — making records irrefutable, transactions frictionless, and asset value protected for life. Safety, efficiency, and collaboration guide everything we build.

Our Network

When you join, your counterparties are already here: verified airlines, MROs, OEMs, lessors, regulators, and recyclers on one permissioned network — each working from one shared, trusted record per asset.

Our Standards

Your auditors need more than promises. Block Aero is ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified by the Aviation Suppliers Association Certification Body (Certificate BLOC-001-06-26-27), aligned with the ICAO Trust Framework, and built on ATA, ASA, and AFRA data standards. See our Trust Center for certificates and live compliance status.

Milestones

From First Registry to Implementation Partner

  • 2025–26

    Implementation partner of the AFRA-CAAC Registry

    Block Aero is the official implementation partner of the AFRA-CAAC Registry program — and the only overseas compliant parts management platform. Aircraft (MSN), engine (ESN), and dismantled parts (PART) registries run on Block Aero through the program's voluntary participation phase, aligned with the CAAC's Advisory Circular on aircraft disassembly.

  • 2025

    Registry as a Service goes live

    First go-live of the Block Aero Registry as a Service solution and first pre-sale of AFRA-CAAC Registry Credits to industry leaders.

  • 2025

    First AFRA-CAAC audits completed

    Program audits successfully completed at customer facilities in Europe and North America, including the first CCAR-145 limited disassembly approvals.

  • 2025

    Recognized across the industry

    Block Aero presented at the Boeing airline customer conference in China and grew adoption across the aerospace supply chain — from disassembly specialists to distributors, airlines, and MROs.
