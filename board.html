Meet the Board

Founder, Director & CEO

Jeremiah Todd Siena

A Tokyo-born American aviation entrepreneur with over 16 years of commercial aerospace experience, Todd Siena is an Aviation Week "40 Under 40" executive dedicated to digitizing global aircraft asset management.

Jeremiah Todd Siena serves as Founder, CEO, and Board Signatory of Block Aero Technologies. With over 16 years of hands-on experience executing projects across commercial aerospace manufacturing, MRO (Maintenance, Repair, and Overhaul), aftermarket trading, and aviation finance, Siena has established himself as a forward-thinking leader in aerospace digitalization. Prior to founding Block Aero, he established Avia-Tek, an aviation consulting and trading firm that completed high-value transactions with global airlines, MROs, original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), and private equity investors across the Asia-Pacific region.

Driven by first-hand experience with the inefficiencies, traceability challenges, and fragmented documentation standards of the aerospace supply chain, Siena founded Block Aero in 2017. His goal was to build the industry's first productionized aviation blockchain network and asset management platform, utilizing distributed ledger technology and machine learning to search, synchronize, and secure critical aircraft, engine, and component data across international stakeholders.

Siena is a member of the Airbus STARS Leadership Program and was recognized by Aviation Week & Space Technology in their "Top 40 Under 40 Aerospace Professionals." A frequent keynote speaker, he has presented on aviation blockchain and digital asset tracking at international forums including the ICAO Aviation Blockchain Summit, Paris Air Lab at Le Bourget, the SpeedNews Aerospace Suppliers Conference, and MRO Asia-Pacific.

Arthur Burton "Burt" Pinoli

An international aviation executive with over 30 years of leadership across Delta, Northwest, and Transamerica, Burt Pinoli pioneered historic US–China aviation routes and commercial agricultural partnerships.

Arthur Burton "Burt" Pinoli serves as Director and Secretary on Block Aero's Board of Directors. Pinoli brings over three decades of corporate development, business unit management, and Asia-Pacific operational leadership. Throughout his executive career, he held pivotal postings including Director of China and General Manager of Asia for Delta Air Lines, General Manager of China for Northwest Airlines, and Managing Director of Asia for Transamerica Airlines.

Pinoli played a central role in shaping US–China aviation relations during a period of rapid economic opening. At Northwest Airlines, he spearheaded the launch of the Beijing–Detroit non-stop service in 1996 — the very first non-stop route operated by a U.S. airline into mainland China. Later at Delta Air Lines, he managed business units generating over $100 million in revenue, launched the Shanghai–Atlanta and Seoul–Atlanta direct routes, and negotiated major code-sharing partnerships with China Southern Airlines and China Airlines.

Beyond commercial aviation, Pinoli's career includes a unique US–China business partnership story. While serving at Transamerica Agricultural Development Corporation in the 1980s, he negotiated and implemented the first Sino-U.S. turnkey dairy joint venture — a $10 million "compensation trade" project that involved air-importing milk cows to build foundation herds for dairy farms in Hong Kong and Guangzhou. He went on to serve as a Director of Kowloon Dairy (GZ) Ltd., bridging agricultural development, international trade, and regional transportation logistics.

Board Director

Tom Covella

A legendary component aftermarket executive, Tom Covella revolutionized aircraft MRO supply chains by becoming the first in the industry to dismantle a CFM56 engine for parts.

Tom Covella joined the Board of Directors of Block Aero in March 2025. With more than 30 years of executive experience across commercial aircraft maintenance, engineering, component distribution, and fleet operations, Covella is widely acknowledged as an innovator in the commercial aviation aftermarket.

Covella earned widespread acclaim across the MRO sector as the first pioneer in the aftermarket to dismantle a CFM56 engine specifically for component supply and teardown parts distribution. This bold move established a blueprint for modern engine material management, demonstrating how high-demand engine components could be repurposed to lower maintenance costs and improve fleet availability worldwide.

In 2001, Covella founded his component solutions company, which achieved extraordinary growth before being acquired by STS Aviation Group in 2008. He served for over 14 years as Division President of STS Component Solutions, building an extensive global OEM distribution network and technical sales pipeline before transitioning into a Group Advisory role to focus on strategic growth and technology innovations.

Kim Schulze

A distinguished aviation asset trading and compliance executive, Kim Schulze co-founded KP Aviation and is widely recognized for her leadership in regulatory integrity and corporate philanthropy.

Kim Schulze joined Block Aero's Board of Directors in June 2025. As the co-founder and former Chief Executive Officer (now Principal & Chief Compliance Officer) of KP Aviation, Schulze has spent over two decades shaping the global aftermarket aerospace material and asset management industry.

Under her leadership, KP Aviation grew from a boutique supplier into a major international aerospace powerhouse with facilities in Arizona, Europe, and South Africa, specializing in aircraft teardowns, engine leasing, and inventory distribution. In early 2024, Schulze transitioned into the Chief Compliance Officer role, focusing on expanding global operational compliance, risk management, and regulatory frameworks across international markets.

In addition to her achievements in aerospace asset trading, Schulze is a highly respected philanthropic leader and champion for diversity within the aviation sector. She has consistently prioritized community engagement, corporate social responsibility, and mentorship programs designed to cultivate diverse talent in technical and executive roles across the aerospace industry.

Board Director

Toby Man Hon Chan

A climate-tech venture capitalist and former Macquarie banker with over 16 years of experience, Toby Chan invests in sustainable aviation fuel, hydrogen infrastructure, and clean technology.

Toby Man Hon Chan joined Block Aero's Board of Directors in February 2022. He is a Co-Founder and General Partner at Audacy Ventures, a specialized venture capital firm focused on scaling climate technology, decarbonization, and energy transition solutions across global markets.

Chan's career spans over two decades across private equity, investment banking, and cleantech investing. He began his career at Macquarie Capital, advising on public-private partnerships, utilities, and infrastructure transactions, and later served as a founding team member at Kerogen Capital, an energy private equity fund with over $2 billion in AUM. Throughout his institutional career, he advised on and executed over $15 billion in corporate transactions.

At Audacy Ventures and through his direct board seats, Chan actively backs technologies targeting aviation decarbonization, low-carbon fuels, and sustainable equipment. His investments and advisory roles span liquid hydrogen infrastructure solutions (GenH2), carbon capture systems (Cool Planet Technologies), battery energy storage (Moment Energy), and digital supply chain solutions for commercial aerospace.
