Asset Registries

Trusted Registries for Every Aerospace Asset

A registry is only useful if everyone can trust it. Block Aero runs aerospace asset registries on the Aviation Blockchain Network — proof of ownership, chain of custody, and parts provenance — so buyers, owners, auditors, and authorities all work from one verifiable record. What We Register

Registries Built on Verifiable Records

Every registry entry is anchored to an asset's digital passport on the Aviation Blockchain Network — tamper-evident, traceable, and verifiable by the parties who rely on it.

Proof of Ownership

An authoritative record of who owns an asset and when ownership transferred — referenceable when listing on marketplaces such as ILS, so buyers can verify title in minutes.

Chain of Custody

A continuous, back-to-birth trace of every transfer, installation, and event — the provenance auditors and counterparties need to accept a part without weeks of due diligence.

Aircraft, Engine & Parts

Dedicated registries for aircraft (MSN), engines (ESN), and dismantled parts (PART) — each with its own identity, history, and compliance record.

Registry Manager · Registry as a Service

Run Your Own Registry

Standards bodies, regulators, OEMs, and consortia use Registry Manager to operate trusted registries without building infrastructure. Define what gets registered, who can issue and audit entries, and who can see them.

  • Submit-to-approve workflows for registry item issuers, with endorsement and oversight by auditors and registry managers.
  • Visibility tiers — public, semi-private, or private — set per registry and per record.
  • Free public verification portal so any counterparty can confirm an entry without an account.
  • Revenue-share model per registry item for program operators (RaaS partnerships).

Programs Running on Block Aero Registries

Live Program

The AFRA-CAAC Registry Program

A controlled, verifiable path for used serviceable material recovered from retired aircraft to return to service on China-registered aircraft. Block Aero is the program's implementation partner — and the only overseas compliant parts management platform. Aircraft (MSN), engine (ESN), and dismantled parts (PART) registries run on Block Aero through the program's voluntary participation phase under CAAC Advisory Circular AC-145-FS-017 R1.
